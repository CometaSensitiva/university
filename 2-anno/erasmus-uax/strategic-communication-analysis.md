# Strategic Communication Analysis — UAX / ULAB

A group project for **Dirección Estratégica** at Universidad Alfonso X el Sabio, Madrid, developed during an Erasmus exchange in the ULAB/Sociograph course context.

## Problem and method

The team analysed promotional videos for AI courses and master's programmes. A shared framework used **15 indicators**, including format, content, speakers, objectives, editing, sound, platform, interaction, accessibility, duration, communication style, message clarity, branding and final call to action.

The deliverables combined a written analysis, a workbook with analysis/coding/final-selection sheets, and a presentation in Spanish. Tags were used to structure comparisons and justify the selection of videos for the course exercise.

## What the work demonstrates

Structuring qualitative evidence, applying a shared comparison framework, collaborating in an international group and presenting findings in Spanish.

The project does not establish causal effects on enrolment or marketing performance. Platform engagement counts reflect the time of the original exercise; they are not current measurements. The material inspected does not establish that the team performed physiological measurement or a validated Sociograph experiment.
